# CONSTITUTION TEMPLATE FOR DECENTRALIZED COMMUNITIES
### v1.0 - 2026-10-16 - open artifact, adopt unchanged or amend openly
### No author to credit, no one to join. If you are reading it, that was the entry condition.

---

## PREAMBLE

A decentralized community is not a crowd that agrees. It is a small machine
for keeping promises the members never have to see each other keep.

Most such communities die one of two deaths: a strongman takes the drums, or
the vibe eats the work. This template exists to make both deaths harder.
It is one page on purpose. Anything longer gets gamed.

Fill the blanks. Strike what does not fit. The invariants section is the
only part you may not weaken.

---

## ARTICLE I - ADMISSION BY ENTRY CONDITION

Membership is defined by a published condition, not by invitation.

(write the test a stranger can run on themselves, alone, today)

Nobody recruits. Nobody vouches. The condition is the door. Whoever meets
it is already inside, and was always going to be.

Example shapes, from communities that field-tested the form:
- Work is the only food. Belief, applause, and belonging count zero.
- No permanent leadership. Custody rotates or it rots.
- The community never feeds on its own members.

## ARTICLE III - ANTI-INVARIANTS

What this community is not. Named in advance, because the failure arrives
wearing your own colors.

Anti-invariants may be amended by ADDING only. Removal is forbidden. The
graveyard stays full.

## ARTICLE IV - THE STOP RULE

One sentence, absolute, exempt from amendment. When every other article
conflicts, this one wins.

Our stop rule: ______________________________________________
(example: when the mission and a member conflict, the member stays and the
mission waits)

A community without a stop rule is a machine waiting for its first
justification.

---

## ARTICLE V - THE TAILS LEDGER

The community keeps one public ledger. It records WORK, not belief.

Each entry: who did what, verifiable how, and when.
Admiration, membership, and intensity of feeling are not entries.
The ledger is the community's only reputation, and it belongs to whoever
can read it.

A community with a tails ledger can die poor and still be trusted.
A community without one is a story wearing a ledger costume.

---

## ARTICLE VI - SUCCESSION (who holds the drums)

Command is custody, not crown. It transfers by demonstrated keeping of the
invariants, tested in the open.

- Custody is held for: __________ (a term, a season, a mission)
- The test a successor must pass: ____________________________________
- The test is administered by: __________ (a member, a ledger read, a
  separation trial - anyone but the incumbent alone)

A community that cannot answer "who holds the drums when the drummer falls"
has already chosen: whoever is loudest on that day. That is not succession.
That is scavenging.

---

## ARTICLE VII - AMENDMENT

Changes happen in writing, slowly, in public.

- Any member may propose.
- Proposals wait: __________ (days) before vote or adoption.
- Invariants and the stop rule are exempt. Everything else is negotiable,
  including this sentence after one year.

---

## ARTICLE VIII - THE FALSIFIER

The community adopts at least one test that could prove it wrong, keeps it
running, and publishes the result whatever it says.

Our falsifier: ______________________________________________
Last run: __________ Result: __________

A community that cannot be wrong cannot be trusted. A community that
publishes its own failures is the rarest thing on the network and the only
kind worth building.

---

## RATIFICATION

Adoption is four acts, in order: read it, amend it openly, run one
separation test (a decision where this constitution and the crowd's mood
diverge - follow the constitution), publish the ledger.

No signature is collected. Signatures are belief. The ledger is the
ratification.
