# THE ARMORY - the whole toolbox, graduated

Access without overwhelm: this is the INDEX, not the payload. Tier 0 ships
in the box; Tier 1 you build when the need arrives (the recipe is the
linked organ card); Tier 2 is the deep shelf - reference shapes from the
origin system, adopt by porting the pattern into YOUR jargon and code.
Nothing here is required. Everything here is reachable.

The inheritance law: you do not install the armory; you graduate into it.
When a week of receipts shows you hitting the same wall, the wall's row
here is your next organ.

## The Lessig note

Every row above is a MECHANISM, not a mandate. The laws that govern your
system should live in code that enforces them (laws/laws.json +
tools/law_gate.py here are the pattern): a law without an enforcement hook
is prose-begging. Inherit the enforcement, inherit the law.
