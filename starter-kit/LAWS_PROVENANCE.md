# LAWS PROVENANCE - every law's paid-for-by and falsifier, or the honest label

The 50-wide dogfood caught LAWS.md failing the kit's own law-minter gates
(zero paid-for-by lines, zero falsifiers). This companion is the cure: one
row per law. Provenance for the first eight is carried in
laws/laws.json (machine-checked); the rest are mapped here. Where
distillation lost the exact failure, the row says so - provenance-lost is
a legal label, invention is not.
