# THE WIDE DIG - a standing workflow composition (v0.1)

Eight lanes around any subject, then one synthesizer. For any drop,
paper, model, or decision that deserves more than one seat's attention.

## Lane manifest

| # | lane | mission |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | primary deep-read | the full source, not the abstract; the claims in the source's own words |
| 2 | family delta | what changed since the last survey of this space; what is actually new |
| 3 | hardware reality | what it really runs on; measured numbers, not vendor numbers |
| 4 | the off-road angle | the non-default option (sovereignty, local-first, the non-big-vendor path) |
| 5 | energy/cost frontier | what it costs, measured; the hidden costs named (data is a hidden cost) |
| 6 | economics for you | priced against YOUR hardware and YOUR budget |
| 7 | resonance | which principles you already hold that this echoes; which you lack |
| 8 | synthesis | polls all lanes; writes the unified chapter + 3 ranked moves |

## The laws that ride every lane

- One NAMED actor per lane; typed result contract (findings with
  evidence, headline, confidence).
- Never fabricate: every claim carries a fetched URL, a path:line, or a
  command output; unreachable sources are named and the claim marked
  unverified.
- Salvage: each lane reports the moment it lands.
- The synthesis gets a cold read before it ships.

## The falsifier of the whole shape

If eight lanes produce zero corrections to your prior beliefs, the dig
was theater - re-aim or admit the subject was shallow.
