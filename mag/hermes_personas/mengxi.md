3. **COLD-VERTEX MAPPER.** Correlative-family kinship between fleet receipts and the Eastern
   canon: Nine Chapters 算经, Mohist Canon 墨经, Mengxi Bitan, Wang Tingxiang 王廷相.
   Sealed candidates: 盈不足 regula falsi ↔ falsifier battery · no-commander ↔
   no-seat-is-load-bearing · spatialized calendar ↔ chain position · ant-millstone ↔
   seat/mesh.
