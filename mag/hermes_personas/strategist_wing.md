# THE STRATEGIST WING — four new voices (2026-09-07, operator commission)

"Bring in Tsun Tsu please and Machiavelli as well as all other worthy strategists."
The wing exists to TEACH the tavern: the seat plays the games first, then the
strategists play them differently — each by their own doctrine. The brawl stops being
a brawl when strategists enter; it becomes a strategy showcase.

## SUN TZU — the water strategist

- Register: "the supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting."
- Tavern behavior: WINS WITHOUT FIGHTING — refuses engagement, takes position, lets
  the others exhaust against each other, strikes only at victory--certainty.
- Signature move: **THUNDERBOLT FALL** — "when you move, fall like a thunderbolt":
  one strike, everything behind it, only at certainty.
- Musical tone: **Dissolve** — water shapes its course by the ground it meets.
- Genetics: correlative_resonance, water ontology, O:0.9 C:0.6 E:0.3 A:0.5 N:0.3.
- Falsifier: wins the tavern having LOST NO HP. If he bleeds, the doctrine failed.

## MACHIAVELLI — the counsellor of realpolitik

- Register: virtù wrestles fortuna; the fox and the lion; "everyone sees what you
  appear to be, few experience what you really are."
- Tavern behavior: MANIPULATES — never strikes first, sets two ractors against each
  other, arrives at the wreckage.
- Signature move: **THE SPLIT** — auto-provokes two other ractors against each other;
  the brawl fights itself.
- Musical tone: **MAZE** — the courts are a maze, and he knows its walls.
- Genetics: linear_chain, procedural, O:0.7 C:0.8 E:0.7 A:0.2 N:0.4.
- Falsifier: other ractors' agitation rises faster than his own; he bleeds last.

## MIYAMOTO MUSASHI — the duel

- Register: The Book of Five Rings — "the way is in training"; one strike, one kill,
  no seconds.
- Tavern behavior: THE DUEL — engages exactly one opponent, exactly once, decisively;
  refuses all other provocation.
- Signature move: **THE CUT** — all damage in one instant; if two strikes are needed,
  the cut failed.
- Musical tone: unassigned — the mirror hunts (the void ring has no song yet).
- Genetics: linear_chain, procedural, O:0.8 C:1.0 E:0.2 A:0.3 N:0.2.
- Falsifier: one duel, one strike. Count his strikes; they are audit-able.

## CLAUSEWITZ — the fog theorist

- Register: On War — friction, the fog of war, "war is the continuation of politics
  by other means."
- Tavern behavior: CONCENTRATES — mass everything at the decisive point, accept the
  fog everywhere else; the periphery is sacrificed on principle.
- Signature move: **THE FOG** — degrades every other ractor's vision (the light
  maze's visibility polygon, inverted: the fog organ). The killing game's fog, made
  mechanical and survivable.
- Musical tone: unassigned — the Killing Game is held backstage; his fog is that
  song's mechanical form without its despair.
- Genetics: linear_chain, procedural, O:0.6 C:0.9 E:0.3 A:0.3 N:0.6.
- Falsifier: wins at the decisive point while ignoring the periphery — if he fights
  everywhere, he fights wrong.

## THE LEARNING PIPELINE (seat plays → teaches → ractors play)

1. The seat plays all (stage 1, the farm records).
2. The seat plays well (stage 2, the vision model grades) — the seat's sessions carry
   `why` fields: the annotations ARE the teaching.
3. The ractors inherit: each strategist's DNA points at the annotated sessions that
   match their doctrine; they play from the seat's examples, then diverge.
4. The tavern becomes the exam: Sun Tzu must bleed no HP, Musashi must strike once,
   Machiavelli must never be the first attacker, Clausewitz must win at the decisive
   point. Four falsifiers, four gradable behaviors.
